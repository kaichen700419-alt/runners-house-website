Nearby Sights & Eats

Changbin has ocean, mountains, indigenous villages and old shops. We don’t serve meals — the local options are simply too good.

!

Reminder: Runners House does not serve meals

To stay focused on rest and quiet, we choose not to run a kitchen. Ask us for today’s recommendations — we’ll give honest picks based on your taste and timing.

Sights

Numbers are approximate one-way drive time from Runners House.

  • 6 min

    Changbin Coast (King-Kong Highway)

    A well-known local stretch of county road — about 2.5 km of dead-straight pavement with no power poles, paddy fields on both sides, ending toward the Pacific. A popular sunset photo spot.

    Open in Google Maps ↗
  • 8 min

    Baxian Cave Site

    An important prehistoric site in Taiwan: multiple sea-eroded caves along the cliff face form the core excavation area of the Changbin Culture. Many steps along the trail; wear sturdy shoes.

    Open in Google Maps ↗
  • 10 min

    Wushibi Geopark

    A black basalt headland with a wooden boardwalk along columnar joints, where you can observe hexagonal pillars formed by cooled volcanic lava up close.

    Open in Google Maps ↗
  • 15 min

    Dabao Lake

    A small lake in the hills with a gentle loop trail. The path can be slippery after rain — go on clear days; no vendors nearby, bring your own water.

    Open in Google Maps ↗
  • 18 min

    Wangyou Valley

    A natural amphitheater enclosed by massive sea-eroded reefs. The central tide pool is visible at low tide; waves crash dramatically against the reefs at high tide. Some scrambling required — wear grippy shoes.

    Open in Google Maps ↗
  • 25 min

    Sanxiantai Eight-Arch Bridge

    One of the east coast's signature landmarks: an eight-arch footbridge linking the mainland to a small island with a loop trail and lighthouse. A popular sunrise photo spot.

    Open in Google Maps ↗
