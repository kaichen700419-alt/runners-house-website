III CHAPTER · A RUNNER’S HOME

Runners

More than a bed — a runner-savvy stopover: wash your kit, sleep well, and hit the road tomorrow.

RUN · Home Base

About the runners theme

The host is a long-distance runner. The B&B was rebuilt from an abandoned rice barn and sits beside Highway 11, near several Hualien-Taitung marathon courses — naturally a stop for traveling runners.

Nearby races

The B&B lies along several race routes. Information only — please refer to each organizer's announcement for the latest details.

  • 01
    May

    Changbin King-Kong Marathon

    42K（全馬）／ 21K（半馬）／ 11K（健康組）

    An iconic spring marathon on Taiwan’s Pacific coast, held every May along the Changbin stretch of Highway 11; the course runs north-south past landmarks such as the King-Kong Highway, Wushibi, and Baxian Cave. Please refer to the organizer for the latest details.

    More info ↗
  • 02
    Oct

    Sanxiantai Marathon

    42K（全馬）／ 21K（半馬）／ 9K（健康組）

    A sunrise-themed autumn race held every October around Sanxiantai; the start is intentionally set roughly 30 minutes before sunrise, so runners catch the first light from the eight-arch bridge. Gentle elevation and continuous ocean views — a friendly first half- or full-marathon. Please refer to the organizer for the latest details.

    More info ↗

Possible morning run directions

The Pacific coast on Highway 11 is steps away. Routes below are terrain references only; please assess actual conditions and your own fitness on the day.

  • Coast direction (short)

    Distance
    約 5 公里（單程 2.5K，原路折返）
    Elevation
    平緩
    Difficulty
    Easy

    Heads north along the Highway 11 coastal stretch — gentle terrain with continuous ocean views, suitable for morning or recovery runs. Please assess actual conditions based on weather and your own state.

    Open in maps ↗

  • Inland fields direction (medium)

    Distance
    約 10 公里
    Elevation
    起伏小
    Difficulty
    Moderate

    Heads inland through Changbin's rice paddies before returning along the coast. Gentle backroads first, coastal return after. Please assess actual conditions based on weather; can be slippery after rain.

    Open in maps ↗

  • Advanced coast direction (long)

    Distance
    約 15 公里
    Elevation
    中等爬升
    Difficulty
    Hard

    A longer southbound coastal direction with moderate climbs and several headland turns, suitable for advanced weekend long runs. No on-route refuel — bring water and supplies. Please assess actual conditions based on weather and your own state.
