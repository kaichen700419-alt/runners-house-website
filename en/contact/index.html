Frequently asked questions

Ten of the questions our host hears most often. For anything else, please ask via LINE.

  • What are the check-in and check-out times?

    Check-in is 15:00 to 21:00 on the day of arrival; check-out is by 11:00 the next morning. If you expect to arrive after 21:00, please notify the host via LINE in advance.

  • Is a deposit required to book?

    Yes — a 50% deposit confirms the reservation. The cancellation policy is available on request via LINE when booking.

  • Do you serve breakfast or any meals?

    We do not serve breakfast or meals. Changbin has many local eateries — the host is happy to recommend based on your taste and schedule.

  • Are laundry facilities available?

    Yes — coin-operated washer and dryer are available in the public area for multi-night stays or post-exercise cleanup.

  • Do the rooms come with a kitchen?

    Some room types include a simple mini-kitchen for self-cooking, but not all of them. Please confirm room-specific availability via LINE when booking.

  • Do you offer Tesla charging? Is it free for guests?

    We offer Tesla Wall Connector (dual-cable) plus TPC and Type 2 connectors as a paid service. The same rate applies to guests and non-guests — there is no guest discount or complimentary plan — and a reservation is required.

  • Are pets allowed?

    To preserve room cleanliness and the experience of other guests, pets are not permitted anywhere on the property.

  • Do you provide single-use amenities (toothbrush, razor, comb)?

    In keeping with our sustainability commitments, we do not provide single-use amenities. Please bring your own toothbrush, razor, comb, and similar personal items.

  • How do I get to Runners House?

    We are at 962 No. 47, Zhuhu Village, Changbin Township, Taitung — beside KM 91 on Highway 11. About 90 min drive south from Hualien, 75 min north from Taitung City. By transit, take the coastal bus to "Zhuhu" stop. Free courtyard parking.

  • How do I book or get in touch?

    LINE Official @659gkrae is the fastest channel and is recommended for bookings. You may also call the host at +886 933 430 418, the secondary line +886 912 699 709, or use the email form on the contact page.
