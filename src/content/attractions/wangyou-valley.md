  一片由數塊巨型海蝕岩礁圍成的天然海岸劇場，藏在台 11 線南段一處不起眼的小路旁。退潮時可走進礁岩中央的天然池塘，看小魚與螃蟹被困在水窪裡；漲潮時白浪打在礁岩上聲勢驚人。是進階海岸 15K 跑步路線的折返點。需小心攀爬，請穿防滑鞋。
description_en: >-
  A natural amphitheater enclosed by massive sea-eroded reefs, tucked off a small lane on the south Highway 11 stretch. At low tide you can walk to the central tide pool to see fish and crabs in the puddles; at high tide the waves slam the reefs spectacularly. Turnaround for the Advanced Coast 15K route. Some scrambling required — wear grippy shoes.
