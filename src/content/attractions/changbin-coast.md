description_en: >-
  One of Taiwan's most beautiful county roads. Six minutes south from Runners House you'll reach the King-Kong Highway: a 2.5 km dead-straight stretch with no power poles, paddies on both sides, ending in the Pacific. The starting line of the Changbin King-Kong Marathon — and the hottest sunset photo spot. Plan 30 minutes to walk a section.
