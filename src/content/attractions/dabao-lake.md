  隱藏在山林間的小型堰塞湖，當地人秘境。湖面靜謐如鏡，倒映四周低海拔闊葉林；環湖步道約 1.5 公里，平緩好走。湖邊偶有藍腹鷴與大冠鷲出沒，賞鳥族群必訪。雨後路滑，建議天氣晴朗時前往。沒有商家，請自備飲水。
description_en: >-
  A small landslide-dammed lake hidden in the hills — a local secret. The mirror-still water reflects the low-altitude broadleaf forest; the loop trail is gentle and about 1.5 km. Swinhoe's pheasant and crested serpent eagle have been spotted along the shore — a must for birders. Slippery after rain; go on clear days. No vendors — bring water.
