description_zh: >-
  由黑色玄武岩構成的海岬地形，因外型像烏黑鼻頭得名。海邊木棧道沿玄武岩柱狀節理鋪設，可近距離觀察 600 萬年前火山熔岩冷卻形成的六角柱。退潮時可走到岸邊潮間帶看寄居蟹與藤壺。是海岸線 5K 晨跑路線的折返點。
description_en: >-
  A black basalt headland — its name means "black-stone nose" for the shape. A wooden boardwalk runs alongside columnar joints where 6-million-year-old volcanic lava cooled into hexagonal pillars. At low tide, walk down to the intertidal zone for hermit crabs and barnacles. Also the turnaround for the Coast 5K sunrise route.
