description_zh: >-
  以日出為主題的秋季賽事，每年 10 月於三仙台周邊舉辦。起跑時間刻意安排在日出前 30 分鐘，跑者可在跨海八拱橋上看到太平洋第一道曙光。賽道平緩、海景連續，是初次挑戰半馬／全馬的友善選擇。跑者之家為三仙台馬官方合作補給點之一，並提供賽後接駁與裝備寄放。
description_en: >-
  A sunrise-themed autumn race held every October around Sanxiantai. The starting horn deliberately falls 30 minutes before sunrise so runners catch first light from atop the eight-arch bridge. Gentle elevation, continuous ocean views — a friendly first half- or full-marathon. Runners House is an official partner refuel point and offers post-race shuttle plus gear storage.
