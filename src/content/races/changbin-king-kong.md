description_zh: >-
  太平洋海岸線爆發力最強的馬拉松，每年 5 月在台 11 線長濱段舉辦。賽道沿海岸線南北延伸，沿途可看到金剛大道、烏石鼻、八仙洞等地標；補給站豐富、海風強勁，是台灣東海岸跑者最愛的春季賽事之一。跑者之家為主場合作住宿，住客可獲得賽事優惠並安排賽前晨跑領跑。
description_en: >-
  The most explosive marathon on Taiwan's Pacific coast, held every May along the Changbin stretch of Highway 11. The course runs north-south past landmarks like the King-Kong Highway, Wushibi, and Baxian Cave. Generous aid stations, strong sea winds — one of east Taiwan's most beloved spring races. Runners House is an official partner stay; guests get event discounts and a pre-race sunrise pace-leader option.
